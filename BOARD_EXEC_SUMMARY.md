# Year Ahead v2 Executive Summary (Revised)

## Product identity (for alignment)

### Primary identity
**Values alignment mirror**

Year Ahead helps users see whether how they spend time matches how they want to live.

### Supporting identities
- strategic planning layer
- burnout risk signal

### Not primary in v2
- life coaching / existential interpretation system

---

## Positioning language update

Board feedback suggested tightening "feel your year."

Recommended phrasing:
- **See the shape of your year**
- **Design the rhythm of your year**

---

## What stays in v2

1. **Year-first hierarchy**
   - the year canvas is the hero
   - admin controls move into settings/profile

2. **Zoom continuum**
   - Year Pulse -> Month Focus -> Day/Edit
   - continuous transitions, minimal context loss

3. **Signal over decoration**
   - visual density with readable continuity
   - reduced ornamental noise

4. **10-second interpretability test**
   - keep as hard quality gate

---

## What changes now (critical)

1. **Load model must be transparent and hybrid**
   - inferred baseline + user-adjustable weights + lightweight calibration

2. **Recovery cannot equal emptiness**
   - support explicit restorative markers

3. **Insight tone must preserve agency**
   - observational language, not moral scoring

4. **Trust architecture is mandatory**
   - users must see what is inferred, what is entered, and how to adjust

---

## Absolute v2 priorities

- Year hierarchy refactor
- Zoom continuity scaffold
- Visual seam and density cleanup
- ONE transparent pressure layer
- Gentle, observational insight strip

---

## Defer for later

- deep coaching prompts
- heavy narrative logic
- advanced seasonal and physiological systems
- complex category taxonomy expansion

---

## v2 success test

Within 10 seconds, users can answer:

1. Where pressure appears highest
2. Where restoration appears low
3. Whether distribution seems intentional

And they trust the model enough to understand "how this was inferred."
